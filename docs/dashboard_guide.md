# 📚 Analyst Guide: NIFTY 100 Financial Intelligence Platform

Welcome to the NIFTY 100 Financial Intelligence Platform! This guide will help analysts and users navigate the dashboard, apply filters, and export financial data effectively.

---

## 🧭 1. Navigation (How to move around)
We use the **Sidebar** on the left side of the dashboard to navigate to different modules. The application primarily features 9 analytical screens:

1. **Company Profile:** To view basic company information, sector details, and corporate business overviews.
2. **Price Trends:** To evaluate stock price movements and Moving Averages using interactive line charts.
3. **Returns Analysis:** To analyze daily/monthly returns distributions using Histograms and Box Plots.
4. **Financial Ratios:** To examine critical financial Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) like P/E, ROE, etc.
5. **Peer Comparison:** To compare a company against other competitors within the same industry using Radar Charts.
6. **Sector Analysis:** To view sector-wise median KPIs and interactive bubble charts.
7. **Capital Allocation:** To understand corporate capital allocation patterns using a Treemap visualization.
8. **Annual Reports:** To find the official BSE PDF links of companies' annual reports.
9. **Valuation & Market Data:** To identify and rank overvalued (Caution) and undervalued (Discount) stocks.

---

## 🔍 2. Filtering Data (How to filter)
Highly user-friendly filtering options are integrated across the dashboard to help you extract exact datasets:

* **Sidebar Dropdowns:** Most screens include a 'Filter Controls' section on the left. First, select the required industry via **'Select Sector'** (e.g., IT, Financials). After that, use **'Select Company'** to filter down to a specific organization.
* **Date Range Pickers:** In screens like Price Trends and Returns Analysis, you can filter data dynamically by choosing customized timeframes (Start Date & End Date).
* **Sliders:** In the Valuation screen, an interactive slider can be utilized to filter companies based on a dividend threshold (e.g., Minimum 2% Yield).

---

## 📥 3. Exporting Data (How to download)
Analyzed financial metrics can be easily downloaded to your local computer as Excel-friendly CSV files.

* **Valuation & Market Data (Screen 09):** There is a dedicated section located at the very bottom called **"📥 Download Reports"**.
* Clicking on **"Download Valuation Summary (CSV)"** will instantly export the entire comparative listing ranked by FCF & Dividend Yield.
* Clicking on **"Download Valuation Flags (CSV)"** will generate a separate file containing only the companies tagged under overvalued (Caution) or undervalued (Discount) brackets.
